Privacy 🔏

Plain-English summary of what we collect, why, and your choices.

1) Who we are

AstroRealm is an entertainment website that offers astrology-style readings. Access to some readings is unlocked after completing a simple task with a third-party provider (e.g., CPAlead).

2) Data we collect

  • Basic usage data: standard logs (IP, device/browser info) from your visit—used to run and protect the site.
  • Reading inputs (optional): the text you type in forms (e.g., your question, sign). Our current site runs with no JavaScript, so we do not store this data in your browser by script. Your browser may remember form entries if you enable that in your settings.
  • Palmistry photos (optional): when you choose a file on the Palmistry page, it stays on your device unless you email or share it yourself. We do not upload it.
  • Contact messages: if you use the email link/form on the Contact page, your email client sends the message to us; we receive what you send.

3) Offerwall/content locker

When you click “Unlock Your Reading,” you are redirected to a third-party provider to complete a task. That provider operates under its own privacy policy and may set cookies or collect information needed to deliver and verify the offer. After completion, they redirect you back to AstroRealm.

Tip: you can block third-party cookies in your browser settings if you prefer, but some offers may not work without them.

4) How we use your data

  • Provide and improve the site’s features and appearance.
  • Show the right pages after you complete a task (unlock flow).
  • Protect the site from abuse or technical issues.

5) Storage & retention

  • Your device: because the site is static with no JavaScript, we don’t write to local storage. Any drafts you previously saved in older versions may appear under a key like astrorealm_draft (legacy). You can clear site data in your browser to remove it.
  • Server logs: minimal logs are kept for security and reliability, then routinely cycled.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

6) Your choices

  • Use the site without uploading a palm photo—this feature is optional.
  • Control cookies and trackers via your browser settings.
  • Clear any legacy saved data by removing site data in your browser.

7) Children

AstroRealm is intended for users aged 13+ (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a minor has provided data to us, please contact us to request deletion.

8) International users

Our site may be accessed globally. By using AstroRealm, you understand your information may be processed where our hosting or partners operate.

9) Changes to this policy

We may update this page to reflect technical or legal changes. Material updates will be noted here with a new “Last Updated” date.

10) Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@astrogate.site.
